8. Troubleshooting and FAQ

Dashboard

Variance says "Not available". No eligible order has a comparable cost yet. A cost is comparable only if it is Invoice-final or Merchant-confirmed, Tax-exclusive, and in the order's currency. See key ideas.

An order I edited is missing from the report. Check that it is Processing or Completed, has a shipping line, and was created inside the date range. The date is the order creation date, not the shipping date.

Confirmed cost is lower than the costs I entered. Booked-label, estimate and other-currency costs are not counted. Export the CSV and look at Comparison status.

"The selected dates were invalid or exceeded 366 days." Choose a range of 366 days or less, with From before To.

"N confirmed cost record(s) use a different currency…" Those orders have a cost in another currency. They are kept but not totalled.

The dashboard is blank. Check that WooCommerce is active and your browser has JavaScript enabled. The page needs WordPress's built-in React (wp-element), which another plugin can break; try with other plugins deactivated.

Order editor

My cost disappeared after saving. The amount was not accepted. Use digits and a period only: 12.50, not 12,50, $12.50 or -12.50.

I want to delete a cost. Empty Merchant-paid shipping cost and click Update. A private note records the removal.

FAQ

Does it change my shipping rates or order totals? No. It only adds cost records and notes.

Does it work with HPOS? Yes. All reads and writes use WooCommerce order APIs and work with both HPOS and legacy storage.

Is my data sent anywhere? The free plugin contacts no external service. Pro contacts ShipStation only after you connect it, and only with the request described in chapter 7.

Can I record costs per parcel? Not in this version; one cost per order. Add parcel costs together.

Does it convert currencies? No, by design. See rule 3.
